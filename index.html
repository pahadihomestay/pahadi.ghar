Welcome to The Pahadi Ghar - Darcha Nestled amidst the serene and majestic landscapes of the Lahaul Valley, The Pahadi Ghar is more than just a homestay—it’s a mountain experience. Surrounded by towering peaks and crisp, pristine skies, we offer the comfort of a home blended with authentic Himalayan hospitality. If you are looking to escape the daily hustle, breathe in the fresh mountain air, and unwind in nature's lap, welcome to your second home in the hills.

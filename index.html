Travel Guide

How to Reach Darcha

Plan your journey across spectacular mountain passes and scenic highways to reach our doorstep.

By Road

Drive or take a bus along the Manali-Leh Highway via the magnificent Atal Tunnel and Jispa. Well-connected for cars, SUVs, and bikes.

By Air

Fly into Bhuntar Airport (Kullu), located approximately 150 km away. From the airport, hire a taxi or take local transport via Manali.

By Rail

Chandigarh Railway Station is the nearest major railhead. From Chandigarh, scenic road trips via Mandi and Manali lead up to Darcha.
